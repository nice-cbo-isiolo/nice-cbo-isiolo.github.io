Official organisational information

Who we are

Northern Inclusion Community Empowerment (NICE) CBO is a community-based organisation established to advance inclusive development and empower underserved communities.

Our work is guided by a commitment to community participation, equity, capacity building, economic empowerment and sustainable development.

We welcome engagement with individuals and institutions interested in understanding our organisational identity, objectives and development priorities.
