OUR PROGRAMMES

Turning community priorities into opportunities for change.

We seek to advance inclusive development through practical initiatives that strengthen people, improve livelihoods, protect the environment and expand opportunities for underserved communities.

Our development focus

Responding to community needs. Creating pathways to opportunity.

NICE's programme priorities are guided by the belief that sustainable development must include everyone. We aim to work with communities, public institutions, civil society and development partners to identify local challenges and develop appropriate responses.

Our thematic areas provide a framework for identifying programme opportunities, mobilising technical expertise, developing partnerships and designing interventions that respond to the realities of communities in Isiolo and the wider Northern Kenya region.

Important: The areas below represent NICE's programme priorities and potential interventions. They are not a claim that all programmes are currently operational or funded. Specific projects will be developed according to identified needs, organisational capacity, available resources and partnerships.

Thematic areas

Our nine programme priorities

Each priority offers opportunities for collaboration, project development and community-led action.

🌿

1. Environmental Sustainability & Climate Resilience

Promoting environmental responsibility, sustainable natural resource management and community resilience to environmental and climate-related challenges.

Potential interventions

  • Community environmental awareness and education.
  • Tree growing and locally appropriate restoration initiatives.
  • Community-led waste management and environmental cleanliness.
  • Climate change awareness and resilience building.
  • Water conservation and sustainable resource use.

Intended contribution: More environmentally responsible communities and improved capacity to respond to climate and environmental challenges.

💡

2. Youth Development & Empowerment

Supporting young people to develop skills, confidence, leadership abilities and pathways towards meaningful economic and social participation.

Potential interventions

  • Life skills and employability training.
  • Entrepreneurship and enterprise development support.
  • Youth mentorship and leadership development.
  • Digital literacy and innovation opportunities.
  • Participation in community development and civic activities.

Intended contribution: Better access to skills, knowledge, leadership opportunities and livelihood pathways for young people.

🌸

3. Women's Economic Empowerment

Advancing women's participation, leadership and economic independence through opportunities for skills development, enterprise and sustainable livelihoods.

Potential interventions

  • Entrepreneurship and business skills development.
  • Financial literacy and savings awareness.
  • Support for women-led income-generating initiatives.
  • Leadership and decision-making capacity building.
  • Access to markets, networks and economic opportunities.

Intended contribution: Stronger economic participation, confidence and livelihood opportunities for women.

♿

4. Disability Inclusion

Promoting the rights, dignity, accessibility and meaningful participation of persons with disabilities in community and development processes.

Potential interventions

  • Disability awareness and community sensitisation.
  • Inclusive participation in community programmes.
  • Promoting accessibility and barrier-free environments.
  • Capacity building for disability-inclusive development.
  • Partnerships with organisations of persons with disabilities.

Intended contribution: More inclusive communities where persons with disabilities can participate and access opportunities with dignity.

🎓

5. Education & Capacity Building

Strengthening knowledge, practical skills and institutional capacity as foundations for individual growth and sustainable community development.

Potential interventions

  • Community learning and awareness initiatives.
  • Mentorship and life skills development.
  • Training for community groups and local organisations.
  • Digital and basic information technology skills.
  • Organisational management and leadership training.

Intended contribution: Improved knowledge, practical capabilities and stronger community-level institutions.

🧭

6. Governance, Advocacy & Social Inclusion

Encouraging informed participation, community dialogue, accountability and awareness of rights and responsibilities.

Potential interventions

  • Community awareness and civic education.
  • Public participation and community dialogue.
  • Rights awareness and social inclusion activities.
  • Community feedback and accountability initiatives.
  • Constructive engagement between communities and institutions.

Intended contribution: Better-informed communities and stronger participation in local development processes.

🌾

7. Sustainable Livelihoods

Promoting practical economic opportunities that strengthen household resilience, encourage enterprise and support sustainable income generation.

Potential interventions

  • Small enterprise and entrepreneurship development.
  • Skills training linked to livelihood opportunities.
  • Support for appropriate community-based enterprises.
  • Market awareness and business development.
  • Climate-resilient livelihood approaches.

Intended contribution: Improved livelihood opportunities and stronger economic resilience among participating households.

🫶

8. Children & Vulnerable Groups

Promoting the wellbeing, protection, dignity and inclusion of children and vulnerable individuals through community-based approaches and appropriate partnerships.

Potential interventions

  • Child protection awareness and safeguarding education.
  • Community awareness on children's rights and wellbeing.
  • Identification and referral through appropriate service providers.
  • Inclusive learning and community support initiatives.
  • Awareness on protection from abuse, neglect and exploitation.

Intended contribution: Greater awareness of protection needs and improved community support for children and vulnerable groups.

🤲

9. Community-led Development

Strengthening community participation and local ownership in identifying development challenges, setting priorities and shaping sustainable solutions.

Potential interventions

  • Community needs assessments and consultations.
  • Participatory planning and priority setting.
  • Capacity building for community groups.
  • Community-led initiatives and local partnerships.
  • Monitoring, feedback and learning activities.

Intended contribution: Stronger community ownership, collaboration and locally responsive development initiatives.

02 Disability inclusion

Considering accessibility and participation for persons with disabilities.

03 Community ownership

Involving local people in identifying needs and shaping solutions.

04 Safeguarding

05 Environmental responsibility

From priorities to action

How we intend to develop our programmes

NICE seeks to follow a practical and participatory approach that connects community priorities with appropriate expertise, resources and partnerships.

1. Identify community needs

Engage community members and relevant stakeholders to understand local challenges, opportunities and development priorities.

2. Design responsive interventions

Translate identified needs into realistic project concepts with clear objectives, activities, expected outcomes and implementation arrangements.

3. Build partnerships and mobilise resources

Seek collaboration with development partners, public institutions, technical organisations and other stakeholders whose expertise and resources can support programme objectives.

4. Implement with communities

Work with participating communities and partners to deliver agreed activities while promoting inclusion, accountability and local ownership.

5. Monitor, evaluate and learn

Track progress, document results, gather feedback and apply lessons to improve programme quality and inform future interventions.
