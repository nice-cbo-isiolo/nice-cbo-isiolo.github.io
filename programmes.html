WHAT WE FOCUS ON

Programmes that put people and communities first.

Our thematic areas guide practical, inclusive initiatives shaped by community priorities and partnerships.

Thematic areas

Our programme priorities

These are areas of focus, not a claim that every programme is already active. Activities will be developed as needs, capacity and partnerships are established.

🌿

Environmental Sustainability & Climate Resilience

Community environmental awareness, sustainable natural resource management, restoration and locally appropriate climate resilience action.

💡

Youth Development & Empowerment

Skills development, mentorship, leadership, civic participation, innovation and pathways to decent livelihoods.

🌸

Women’s Economic Empowerment

Women’s leadership, participation, skills, entrepreneurship and livelihood opportunities.

♿

Disability Inclusion

Accessibility, awareness, inclusive services and participation of persons with disabilities.

🎓

Education & Capacity Building

Community learning, life skills, mentorship and training that strengthen individual and organisational capacity.

🧭

Governance, Advocacy & Social Inclusion

Informed participation, accountability, community dialogue and awareness of rights and responsibilities.

🌾

Sustainable Livelihoods

Inclusive livelihood initiatives, entrepreneurship and practical economic opportunities with local partners.

🫶

Children & Vulnerable Groups

Community approaches that promote child wellbeing, protection, dignity and inclusion.

🤲

Community-led Development

Working with residents and stakeholders to identify priorities and shape responsive initiatives.
