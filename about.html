ABOUT NICE

Our story begins with a shared belief.

Communities are stronger when people are included, empowered and able to shape the future they want.

Who we are

Rooted in Isiolo. Connected to Northern Kenya.

Northern Inclusion Community Empowerment (NICE) is a registered community-based organisation based in Tullu Roba, Wabera Ward, Isiolo County, Kenya. NICE was established to provide a community platform that brings people together, responds to local challenges and promotes practical opportunities for underserved groups.

Our headquarters and operational base are in Isiolo County, while our wider vision is to build relationships and partnerships that advance inclusion and community development across Northern Kenya and beyond.

Registration authority: Directorate of Social Development, Kenya
Registration No.: DSD/11/49/02/240218
Date registered: 17 August 2026

OUR VISION

A just, inclusive, and prosperous society where every individual and community has equal opportunities to thrive.

Our mission
To empower marginalized and underserved communities through inclusive development, capacity building, economic empowerment, education, advocacy, and sustainable livelihood initiatives.

Our journey

From an idea to a collective vision.

The idea takes shape

The initial idea was developed by the current Chairperson and Vice Chairperson around the need for a community platform to bring people together and respond to local challenges.

Members come together

They mobilised people who shared the vision. Members met to discuss the organisation’s name, objectives, activities and contributions.

A wider identity emerges

An initial county-focused name was considered. Members embraced Northern Inclusion Community Empowerment (NICE), reflecting a broader vision while retaining Isiolo as the organisation’s base.

Formal registration

Following member agreement and collective effort, NICE was formally registered on 17 August 2026.

Building for the future

NICE is strengthening its systems, developing partnerships, building capacity and identifying community programmes grounded in local needs.

From an idea shared by two people, to a collective vision embraced by members, NICE continues to grow with the belief that inclusion creates opportunities and collective action can transform communities.
