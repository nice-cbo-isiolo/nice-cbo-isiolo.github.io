ABOUT NICE

Our story begins with a shared belief.

Communities are stronger when people are included, empowered and able to shape the future they want.

Who we are

Rooted in Isiolo. Connected to Northern Kenya.

Northern Inclusion Community Empowerment (NICE) is a registered community-based organisation based in Tullu Roba, Wabera Ward, Isiolo County, Kenya. NICE was established to provide a community platform that brings people together, responds to local challenges and promotes practical opportunities for underserved groups.

We believe sustainable development begins when communities participate in identifying their priorities, shaping solutions and taking ownership of the changes that affect their lives.

Our headquarters and operational base are in Isiolo County, while our wider vision is to build relationships and partnerships that advance inclusion and community development across Northern Kenya and beyond.

Registration authority: Directorate of Social Development, Kenya
Registration No.: DSD/11/49/02/240218
Date registered: 17 August 2026

OUR VISION

To create an empowered, inclusive, prosperous, and sustainable society where every individual has equal opportunities to realize their full potential.

Our mission
To promote economic empowerment, social inclusion, education, advocacy, environmental sustainability, and community development through innovative and participatory approaches.

Our purpose

What we seek to achieve

NICE works towards practical, inclusive and community-driven development that expands opportunities and strengthens the capacity of individuals and communities.

01 Promote inclusion and equal opportunities for underserved and marginalised communities.
02 Strengthen the social and economic empowerment of women and young people.
03 Advance the participation, dignity and inclusion of persons with disabilities.
04 Support education, skills development, awareness and community capacity building.
05 Promote environmental conservation, climate resilience and sustainable livelihoods.
06 Build partnerships that mobilise knowledge, resources and opportunities for community development.
Our journey

From an idea to a collective vision.

The idea takes shape

The initial idea was developed by the current Chairperson and Vice Chairperson around the need for a community platform to bring people together and respond to local challenges.

Members come together

They mobilised people who shared the vision. Members met to discuss the organisation’s name, objectives, activities and contributions.

A wider identity emerges

An initial county-focused name was considered. Members embraced Northern Inclusion Community Empowerment (NICE), reflecting a broader vision while retaining Isiolo as the organisation’s base.

Formal registration

Following member agreement and collective effort, NICE was formally registered on 17 August 2026.

Building for the future

NICE is strengthening its organisational systems, developing partnerships, building capacity and identifying community programmes grounded in local needs.

From an idea shared by two people, to a collective vision embraced by members, NICE continues to grow with the belief that inclusion creates opportunities and collective action can transform communities.

How we work

Community-led. Partnership-driven. Impact-focused.

NICE believes that lasting development requires meaningful community participation, strong partnerships and responsible implementation.

01 Listen and understand

Engage communities to understand their needs, priorities, challenges and available local resources.

02 Plan together

Support participatory planning and develop practical interventions informed by community priorities.

03 Build capacity

Promote knowledge, skills, awareness and opportunities that strengthen individual and community capabilities.

04 Collaborate and implement

Work with community groups, public institutions, civil society and development partners to advance shared objectives.

05 Monitor and learn

Encourage monitoring, documentation, feedback and learning to improve programme quality and accountability.

06 Promote sustainability

Encourage local ownership, responsible resource use and solutions that communities can sustain over time.

Our commitment

Responsible leadership and accountability

NICE recognises that public trust, responsible leadership and transparent management are essential to effective community development. We are committed to strengthening organisational governance and maintaining systems that support responsible decision-making and the proper use of resources.

01 Member participation

Promoting collective decision-making and meaningful member involvement in organisational affairs.

02 Transparency

Supporting clear communication, proper documentation and responsible reporting.

03 Financial responsibility

Promoting prudent resource management, proper financial records and responsible use of funds.

04 Integrity

Upholding honesty, fairness, respect and ethical conduct in organisational activities.

05 Learning and improvement

Using feedback, monitoring and lessons learned to strengthen organisational performance.

06 Safeguarding and inclusion

Promoting dignity, equal participation and respect for the rights of the communities we seek to serve.

We are committed to developing and maintaining appropriate policies, procedures and organisational records as NICE grows and expands its partnerships and programmes.
