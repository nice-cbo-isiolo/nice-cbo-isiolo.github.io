We welcome collaboration with organisations, institutions, professionals and community networks committed to inclusive and sustainable development.

Why partner with us

Build locally relevant solutions together.

NICE is a community-based organisation headquartered in Isiolo County with a wider Northern Kenya outlook. We value partnerships that combine community knowledge, technical expertise and shared accountability.

As a newly established organisation, we are strengthening our systems and building relationships with experienced partners. We are transparent about our stage of development and committed to learning, responsible delivery and evidence-based programming.

PARTNERSHIP PRINCIPLE

Meaningful collaboration. Clear roles. Community value.

Effective partnerships are built on trust, mutual respect, inclusion and measurable community benefit.
