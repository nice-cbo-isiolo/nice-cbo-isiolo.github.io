PARTNER WITH NICE

Building partnerships. Expanding opportunities. Transforming communities.

Sustainable community development requires collaboration. NICE welcomes partnerships with donors, development organisations, public institutions, civil society, professionals and community networks committed to inclusive and lasting change.

Why work with NICE

Connecting local priorities with opportunities for meaningful change.

Northern Inclusion Community Empowerment (NICE) CBO is a community-based organisation headquartered in Tullu Roba, Wabera Ward, Isiolo County, Kenya. Our mission is to empower underserved communities through inclusive development, capacity building, economic empowerment, education, advocacy and sustainable livelihood initiatives.

We recognise that effective development begins with understanding the people and communities it seeks to serve. Our approach places community participation, inclusion, collaboration and local relevance at the centre of programme development.

As a newly established organisation, NICE is actively strengthening its institutional capacity and building relationships with experienced development partners. We are open about our stage of development and welcome opportunities to learn, collaborate and grow responsibly.

OUR PARTNERSHIP PROMISE

Shared purpose. Responsible collaboration. Community-centred results.

We seek partnerships grounded in mutual respect, clearly defined responsibilities, transparent communication and a shared commitment to improving community wellbeing.

Shared development priorities

01 Environment and Climate Resilience

02 Youth Development

Starting a partnership

How we can begin working together

We aim to make the initial engagement process clear, practical and responsive to the interests of prospective partners.

1. Start a conversation

Contact NICE to introduce your organisation, area of interest and potential opportunity for collaboration.

2. Identify shared priorities

Discuss relevant community needs, programme priorities, target groups and areas where our objectives may align.

3. Develop a suitable concept

Where there is mutual interest, explore a project concept, proposal or collaboration framework with clear objectives and expected results.

4. Agree on roles and responsibilities

Establish appropriate arrangements covering responsibilities, resources, timelines, accountability and reporting requirements.

5. Work together and learn

Subject to agreed resources and arrangements, implement activities, review progress, document lessons and communicate results responsibly.

Responsible partnership development

Building trust through accountability and continuous improvement.

NICE understands that donors and development partners need confidence that resources are managed responsibly and that supported activities are relevant to the communities they seek to serve.

As our organisation develops, we are committed to strengthening the institutional arrangements needed for responsible programme delivery and effective partnerships.

This includes working towards appropriate governance practices, financial accountability, proper records management, safeguarding, monitoring and evaluation, and transparent communication with partners.

Specific partnership arrangements will be guided by the requirements of the proposed activity, applicable laws and mutually agreed responsibilities.
