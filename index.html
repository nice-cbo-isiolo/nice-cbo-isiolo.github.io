Who we are

A shared vision for stronger, more inclusive communities.

Northern Inclusion Community Empowerment (NICE) is a community-based organisation headquartered in Tullu Roba, Wabera Ward, Isiolo County, Kenya.

We connect communities, partners and practical ideas to address local challenges and expand opportunities for underserved groups. Our work is grounded in the belief that communities should have a meaningful voice in shaping their own development.

Rooted in Isiolo, NICE welcomes collaboration with public institutions, civil society organisations, development partners, private sector actors and other stakeholders working towards inclusive and sustainable development across Northern Kenya and beyond.
