Who we are

A shared vision for stronger, more inclusive communities.

Northern Inclusion Community Empowerment (NICE) is a community-based organisation headquartered in Tullu Roba, Wabera Ward, Isiolo County, Kenya.

We connect communities, partners and practical ideas to address local challenges and expand opportunities for underserved groups. Our outlook is rooted in Isiolo and open to collaboration across Northern Kenya and beyond.
